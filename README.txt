Weekly Meal Planner - phone app (version 20261110102426)

Upload this whole folder to any static host (Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages ...).
Then open the https address on your phone and use "Add to Home Screen".
The folder holds only the app - your recipes live on your phone.
To bring your recipes over: laptop app > Preferences > Download or share backup,
send the file to your phone, then phone app > Preferences > Restore.
