Import recipes

Paste links to recipe pages or upload PDF files. They're added to your library, where you can fix anything that was read imperfectly. Nutrition on imports is an estimate unless the page lists it, so check it when you can. Photos and images can't be read automatically — use “+ Add recipe” for those.

Importing from web links needs the laptop version of this app. Here you can add recipes by hand, import a PDF, or restore a backup from your laptop (Preferences, Back up & restore).

    Your recipes, preferences and plan are stored on this device only — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
