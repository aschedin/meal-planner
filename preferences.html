Preferences

Set these once — every week's plan uses them. Changes save automatically and apply the next time you generate a plan.

Nutrition targets

Defaults follow USDA MyPlate and FDA Daily Values for an adult on a 2,000-calorie diet. They're a starting point — adjust them for your family, and check them against current guidance or your doctor's advice.

Weekly food-group servings (per person)

Nutrients

Protein, fiber, calcium, iron, vitamin C, sugar, sodium and calories are matched to recipe data. Put “limit” in a name (like “Sodium (limit)”) to treat it as a ceiling rather than a goal.

Time limits

Recipes longer than these are left out of your weekly plan.

Ingredients to avoid

One per line (or comma-separated). Any recipe with a matching ingredient is never planned — for example “peanut” also excludes peanut butter.

Restore from a backup
